The Space

Twelve tables, one open kitchen and a hand-painted sign wall that has glowed over the counter since the day the doors opened. Everything here is built at doll-house scale — yet the portions are very real.

Scroll around the diorama: the patio sets, the calligraphy banners, the forest portal in the corner — it all sits on one soft little island of its own.
